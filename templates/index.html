Instructions

Unmute: Click the Unmute button to hear the collective chant, starting with the next chant. It cycles every  s.

Recording:

  • Press the Start button to begin recording at the beginning of the next chant.
  • During live recording, the original reference chant is played rather than the collective chant mixed from uploads, so that you can synchronize your voice with it.
  • Recording stops automatically after 0s, which is a few seconds longer than the chant, if not stopped before then by clicking the Stop button.
  • Allow live recording to continue until it stops automatically, maintaining silence after the reference chant ends, to ensure inclusion in the next mix. (The uploaded recording must have a time duration within  s of the full chant cycle time to help filter out invalid recordings.)

Upload: Click the Upload button to upload your recording to the server so that it can be included in the next mix.

Play/Save/Load: You can Play your recording, Save it to your local computer and Load it back in later.

Available Tokens: A token is required to upload. One may be granted at the beginning of each chant cycle depending on demand; they expire in  s if not used.

Chant Mix Contributors: A list of tokens corresponding to uploaded recordings included in the currently playing chant mix. Contributions by you have a yellow left-border.
