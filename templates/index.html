Instructions

Unmute button: Click the Unmute button to hear the collective chant, starting with the next chant. It cycles every  s. The chant stops until the next chant cycle when recording or playing back your own recording.

Chant Text: A transcription of the reference chant, with blank lines between stanzas indicating a full second pause when reciting them.

Recording button: Click the Rec button to begin recording. During live recording, the reference chant is played with lead-in ticks between stanzas so that you can anticipate the next stanza. Headphones are recommended so as to record in a quiet environment. Click the Stop button to stop recording.

Play/Pause/Resume buttons: You can listen to your own recording, with the option to pause it. Until playback ends or you click Stop, you cannot record and the chant cannot restart.

Save/Load buttons: Click Save to store your own recording on your local computer; click Load to retrieve it later. There is only one audio buffer. Loading or recording displaces whatever is temporarily stored in it.

Upload button: Click the Upload button to upload your own recording to the server so that it can be included in the next mix.

Available Tokens: A token is required to upload. One may be granted at the beginning of each chant cycle depending on demand; they expire in  s if not used.

Chant Mix Contributors: A list of tokens included in the currently playing chant mix. Contributions by you have a green left-border.
